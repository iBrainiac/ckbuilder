## Summary

A detailed, numbers-driven guide to CKB mining profitability as of mid-2026. The article benchmarks six miner models from the Antminer K7 (63.5 TH/s) down to the home-friendly CK-BOX 2 (2.1 TH/s), showing that most operators run at a fiat loss under standard electricity rates — the K7 loses $3.20/day at typical rates despite $4.19 in daily revenue. The guide also reviews six mining pools (flagging BTC Pool's 4% fee as high) and explains how depositing rewards into NervosDAO lets miners offset secondary issuance dilution and preserve their network ownership percentage over time.

## Key Points

- All major miners lose money fiat at electricity above $0.04–0.05/kWh as of May 2026
- The break-even threshold is roughly $0.03–0.04/kWh
- PPS pools offer predictable payouts; PPLNS offers slightly better long-term returns
- NervosDAO deposits are a critical strategy for miners who hold long-term
- Mining is only viable for operators with cheap power, strong CKB price conviction, and disciplined reward management
