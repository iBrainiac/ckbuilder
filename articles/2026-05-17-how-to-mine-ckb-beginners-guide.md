## Summary

A beginner-friendly walkthrough of CKB mining, covering what makes Nervos' proof-of-work setup unique and the practical steps needed to get started. The guide explains the Eaglesong hashing algorithm (custom-built to be ASIC-friendly from day one), the NC-MAX consensus mechanism that adjusts difficulty every 4 hours rather than bi-weekly, and the dual reward system where miners earn both block rewards (halving every 4 years) and a fixed secondary issuance of 1.344 billion CKB annually tied to on-chain storage usage.

## Key Points

- GPU mining is no longer profitable — ASIC hardware is required
- Electricity cost is the decisive factor; rates under $0.10/kWh are needed for any viability
- Neuron wallet is recommended for securing mining rewards
- Steps covered: hardware selection → wallet setup → pool selection → miner configuration → earnings monitoring
- Fire and electrical safety risks are flagged for home miners
